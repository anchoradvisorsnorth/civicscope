The Pool — Privacy Policy

A membership-based pool-management service

Last updated: July 19, 2026

What this covers

"The Pool" is a membership-based sports-pool management service (golf and football picks at app.civicscope.io/pool), operated by a sole proprietor. This policy covers the personal information collected to provide the service to registered members, including the SMS text-message program.

Information we collect

  • Name — to show your picks and standings on the pool board
  • Email address — to send pool notifications (PINs, deadlines, results)
  • Mobile phone number — to send pool text notifications, if you opt in

All information is provided directly by members — text-alert sign-ups happen at app.civicscope.io/pool/sms (registered members only).

SMS / text messaging

  • No mobile information sharing. Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data will not be shared with any third parties.
  • Message frequency varies — typically 1–5 messages per week during an active contest week (pick reminders, deadline notices, results), and none in the off-season.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at the email below.

How information is used

Solely to operate the pool: authenticating picks, showing standings, and sending contest notifications by email or text. Nothing else. We do not advertise, and we never share participant information with anyone outside the pool's operation.

Data storage

Participant information is stored in a private, access-controlled database and is retained only while the pool is active. You can request removal of your information at any time.
