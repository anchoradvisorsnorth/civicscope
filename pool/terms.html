The Pool — Terms & Conditions

A private, recreational group contest among friends

Last updated: July 19, 2026

1. What this is

"The Pool" is a private, invitation-only recreational picks contest run among a small group of personal friends (golf and football picks at app.civicscope.io/golf and /football). It is not a commercial service, is not open to the public, and nothing here is a solicitation.

2. Participation

  • Participation is by personal invitation only and limited to adults (18+).
  • Participants provide a name, email address, and (optionally) a mobile number directly to the organizer.
  • Picks must be submitted before the posted deadline each contest week; late picks are not counted.

3. SMS text program — "The Pool" alerts

  • How you opt in: invited members sign up at app.civicscope.io/pool/sms — you enter your name and mobile number and check an unchecked-by-default consent box that states the program name (The Pool), that messages are recurring and frequency varies, that message and data rates may apply, that consent is not a condition of participation, and that you can reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help. Membership is by personal invitation only; the form is not a public offer.
  • By providing your mobile number and consenting (verbally or in writing) you agree to receive recurring contest notification texts: pick reminders, deadline notices, PINs, and results.
  • Message frequency varies (typically 1–5 messages per active contest week). Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to cancel at any time. Reply HELP for help or contact the organizer at the email below.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Consent to receive texts is not a condition of participating in the pool.

4. Privacy

Participant information is used only to operate the pool and is never shared with third parties for marketing purposes. See the Privacy Policy.
