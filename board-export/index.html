Show your bookmarks bar

In Chrome or Edge press Ctrl + Shift + B (Mac: ⌘ + Shift + B). A bar with your bookmarks appears under the address bar.

Drag this button onto the bookmarks bar

Board Export

Drag it up to the bar and drop it there. Don't click it here — it only works on the BoardDocs page. (If dragging doesn't work: right-click the button → Copy link, then right-click the bookmarks bar → Add page, paste it as the URL and name it anything.)

3

Sign in to BoardDocs

Open your BoardDocs site and sign in with your usual BoardDocs login, the same way you always do.

Nothing here sees or stores your login. The export runs inside your own tab, with your own session, and only sends the meeting documents.

4

Click the Board Export bookmark while on that page

A small panel appears in the bottom-right corner and walks through every meeting — agendas, minutes, each item and its attachments. It takes roughly 15–30 minutes. Leave the tab open; you can keep using other tabs.

When it says Finished — thank you, you're done and can close the tab.

If you close the tab early or the computer sleeps, just go back to BoardDocs and click the bookmark again — it picks up where it left off.
