CivicScope

Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 August 2026

CivicScope builds tools for small municipalities — construction cost estimates, question-answering over a village’s own published documents, and daily record-keeping for a water plant. This page says what we collect, why, and who else touches it.

The short version. We collect what the tools need to work and nothing for advertising. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with advertisers. We use no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Most CivicScope tools can be used without giving us your name at all.

Who we are

CivicScope is operated by Anchor Advisors North LLC, Mishawaka, Indiana. For anything on this page — including a request to see or delete your information — write to info@civicscope.io.

What we collect, and why

If you sign in with Google

Some CivicScope tools are restricted to named people at a municipality we work with. Signing in uses Google, and Google tells us four things about the account you chose: your email address, your name, your profile picture, and a stable account identifier. We store those so we can recognise you next time and show your name on records you create.

We ask Google for nothing else. We cannot read your email, your files, your calendar or your contacts, and we never receive your Google password. You can remove our access at any time from your Google account permissions page.

Signing in successfully does not by itself grant access. Access is granted by the municipality and by us, per person. If you sign in with an account that is not on that list, we record the attempt — the email address, the time, your IP address and your browser’s user-agent string — so the account can be added if it should have been, and so unexpected attempts are visible. That record is kept for security purposes.

Once you are signed in, your browser holds a session cookie named cs_session. It is strictly necessary for sign-in to work, contains no personal information beyond an identifier we issue, cannot be read by scripts, and expires after 30 days. Signing out clears it.

If you ask a question in an “Ask <Village>” tool

We store the text of the question, how many passages it matched and how long it took. We do this so the village can see what its published documents are failing to answer — questions that return nothing are the most useful signal the tool produces. Please do not put personal or sensitive details in a question; it is not the right place for them.

Your question, together with passages retrieved from that municipality’s public documents, is sent to Anthropic to compose the answer. Nothing about your Google account is sent with it.

If you use a cost estimating tool

These are open to anyone and need no sign-in. We store the details you enter about the project — location, project type, size, scope description — and the estimate produced, so the tool can show you results and so we can see how it is being used. The project description you type is sent to Anthropic to produce the estimate.

If you choose to have a report emailed to you, or you use a contact form, we store the email address and any name or organisation you provide and use them to send you that report and to reply. We do not add you to a marketing list from an estimate alone.

If you record readings at a water plant

These tools hold operational records for a public water supply — meter readings, chemical levels, sample results, and the initials or name of the operator who took each reading. That information belongs to the municipality, is part of a record it files with its state regulator, and is retained as long as the municipality needs it for that purpose.

Ordinary website usage

When you use a tool we record a usage record containing a random session identifier, timestamps, the page that referred you and your browser’s user-agent string. Where you arrive through a tracked invitation link, we also record the IP address of that click. We use this to understand whether the tools are working and being used. We do not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or any cross-site tracking.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not disclose it to anyone else except where we are legally required to, or where a municipality is entitled to its own records.

Municipal documents are public records

The ordinances, minutes, agendas and similar documents that these tools search are documents the municipality has already published. Answers are drawn from those documents and link back to them. They are working tools, not legal advice and not an official statement by the municipality — check anything that matters against the source document.

How long we keep things

  • Sign-in records — for as long as you have access, plus a reasonable period afterwards. Sign-in attempt logs are kept for security review.
  • Questions and usage records — kept while they remain useful for improving the tools and reporting to the municipality.
  • Municipal operating records — retained for the municipality, which decides how long its records are kept.
  • Contact details you give us — until you ask us to delete them.

Your choices

  • Ask us what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it — write to info@civicscope.io.
  • Revoke CivicScope’s access to your Google account at any time from your Google account permissions page.
  • Sign out to clear your session cookie.
  • Records a municipality holds about its own operations are the municipality’s — we will forward a request about those to it.

Deleting information we are required to keep as part of a municipality’s regulatory record is not something we can do unilaterally; we will tell you if that is the case.

Children

CivicScope is a tool for municipal staff, contractors and residents dealing with local government. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Data is held with the providers listed above. Access to municipal records is restricted to named accounts. We do not receive or store your Google password, and there is no CivicScope password to steal. If something goes wrong that affects you, we will tell you and the municipality.

Changes

If we change how any of this works, we change this page and update the date at the top.
