What was being tested

Today the Desk emits 14 CSI divisions with one dollar figure each. RYC works in 280 line codes — 03-002 Concrete Flatwork, 01-002 Site Superintendent — and their Summary sheet rolls up from those. Can a plan-reading model populate RYC's own breakdown with quantities and unit rates, so the total is computed in code rather than asserted by the model?

If it can, two problems solve themselves: a division can never disagree with the sum of its own lines, and an estimator can check “roofing 8,400 sf @ $14.50” in seconds — which nobody can do with “roofing $81,000”.

What came back

✓ The vocabulary works. All 60 lines used a valid RYC code in the right division. Division 01 came back as duration × monthly rate — 4 months of superintendent at $8,500 — which is exactly how their sheet models general conditions.
✓ The evidence is real and checkable. Lines cite specified products and spec sections by name: Stego Wrap vapour retarder, Talstar-Professional by FMC, ASTM F1554 anchor rods, AIA A201 §11.3.1 for builders risk. It is reading the project manual, not inventing plausible scope.
× It did not measure anything. 25 lines carried a “quantity”, but the only distinct values are 4, 1 and 5,415 — four months, one each, and the building's own square footage reused as a blanket multiplier. 0 quantities were independently read off a drawing. 35 of 60 lines are lump-sum allowances.
The read: the structural half of the idea is proven — RYC's codes, duration modelling, spec-grounded evidence, and arithmetic moved into code. The measurement half is not. This sample was 30 of 200 pages and skewed toward specifications, which is likely part of it — but three distinct quantity values across 60 lines reads as a capability limit, not a sampling artefact.

What I'd conclude

1. Adopt the vocabulary regardless. Emitting RYC's line codes instead of generic CSI divisions costs almost nothing and lets the takeoff populate their Estimate sheet directly, where their own formulas roll it to Summary. That works today, with or without quantities.

2. Move the arithmetic into code regardless. Even with allowances rather than quantities, a division can no longer disagree with the sum of its own lines. That alone removes the reconciliation failure seen in two of three real runs.

3. Don't assume quantities will come. This probe says the model structures and evidences well but does not measure. Before building on measurement, test it directly: a full-page run with drawing sheets prioritised over specifications, on a project where the true quantities are known.

4. Backtest first, still. None of this can be scored without a baseline — RYC has 43 completed jobs with real contract values and their plan sets still sitting in the job folders.
