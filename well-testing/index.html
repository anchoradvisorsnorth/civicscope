The problem

The readings are the easy part. The retyping is where it goes wrong.

Every day someone walks the round and writes the meter, the tank levels and the residuals on a paper sheet. At the end of the month, somebody retypes all of it into the state's spreadsheet, cell by cell, before the 10th.

That is where the trouble starts. A dose worked by hand from a chart does not match what the state's formula calculates. A tank level that does not add up, a meter digit turned around, a residual in the wrong column: nobody sees it until the month is being typed up, weeks after anyone could have walked back and read it again.

If you look after more than one system, you do all of that once for each of them.

What it does

Three jobs, one set of numbers.

The reading taken at the well is the same number the office reviews and the same number that lands in the report. Nothing is copied by hand in between.

01

Readings on a phone or tablet

Pick your name, tap the well you are standing at, and type what you read off the equipment. The app shows what was pumped and the dose before you submit.

  • Opens in the browser. Nothing to install.
  • Keeps working with no signal and sends when it returns
  • Wells, distribution samples and bacti samples
  • No passwords at the well house
02

Real-time review for the office

The operator-in-charge sees every reading the moment it is submitted: which wells were visited today, what each one pumped, the doses, and anything that needs a second look.

  • A missed day or an idle well is named, not buried
  • An out-of-line dose or a slipped decimal point is flagged
  • Fix an entry from the office, with the reason recorded
  • The whole month on one page
03

The EGLE report, filled in

At month end, press Generate. Well Testing fills the state's own Monthly Operation Report workbook from the month's records and hands you the file.

  • Pumpage, entry points, distribution and bacti
  • The form's own layout and formulas, ready to print
  • A reminder on the 7th with what the month still needs
  • Past reports kept beside the readings behind them
Real-time review

Know what happened at the wells today, without driving out to check.

The review page opens on today. Each well shows when it was last read, what it pumped and its dose. Anything that needs attention sits in one short list, in plain words, with the date and the well.

If you sign the report, you see every reading behind it before you do.

The monthly report

The month you already recorded becomes the report you have to file.

Well Testing does not produce a look-alike. It fills in EGLE's own Monthly Operation Report workbook, in the form's own cells, with the form's own formulas and print layout.

You open it, add your comments, sign it and send it the way you do today. The file you generated and the date you filed stay on record next to the readings.

Caught at the well

It questions a bad number while you can still read it again.

Operators type readings, never calculations. The app does the arithmetic, and it knows what each well normally does.

The meter

A meter that runs backwards

The last reading is on the screen. A number below it is refused before it becomes negative pumpage on the report.

The tanks

A tank that went up with no refill

A refill is recorded as a refill. A tank level that rose without one is questioned, so it never turns into a negative chemical dose.

The sample

Free chlorine above total

That cannot be true, so it is not accepted. It is caught at the well, not discovered when the month is typed up.

The day

Pumpage far outside normal

If a well shows several times its usual day, the app says so and asks you to check that you are on the right well's screen.

The dose

One formula, every time

Gallons pumped, pounds fed and dose are calculated from the readings the same way the state's workbook calculates them.

The record

Corrections never erase

A corrected reading keeps the original and the reason for the change. The history behind a signed report stays intact.

How it works

Set up from the sheets you already use.

1

We set up your system

Send us your daily sheet and your blank monthly report. We enter your wells, your chemical feeds and their strengths, your sampling sites and your crew's names. You do not configure anything.

2

Your crew takes the round on a phone

They open one web address, pick their name and tap the well. When they leave the well house, the round is already on record.

3

You review, generate and file

On the 7th you get an email showing what the month holds and what it still needs. Press Generate, add your comments, sign, and send the report to EGLE as you do today.
